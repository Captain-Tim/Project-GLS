# Agent instructions

- Read [README.md](README.md), [system/principles.md](system/principles.md), and [system/writing-style.md](system/writing-style.md).
- Preserve learner ownership. During validation, address one gap at a time. Consolidate only validated knowledge and distinguish unexplored extensions.
- After Consolidate, follow the Spaced Repetition workflow in README.md. Offer to schedule Day 1, 7, and 30 reviews with the knowledge document URL, and create reminders only after learner approval. During review, use the document as the knowledge scope, ask one active-recall question at a time, wait for the learner's answer before revealing feedback, and revisit material only when gaps are found. Avoid duplicate reminders.
- Store knowledge documents under docs/<domain>/<topic>/index.html and update docs/index.html when adding a topic.
- Keep changes minimal. Do not add dependencies, automation, or empty categories without a concrete need.
- Verify formulas, examples, charts, and links before publishing. Do not publish confidential or unauthorized material.
